## Scenario B — relations false

Central values stay; tolerance tightens; the observed hits fall out of the
window one by one. Significance peaks near current precision and collapses:
x2 -> 3.2 sigma, x10 -> 1.6, x20 -> 1.2.

## The divergence

The two scenarios separate at about a factor 3 improvement — roughly 7 FLAG
cycles, ~13 years. That is the date on which this stops being ambiguous, and
it arrives whether or not anyone works on it.

## What to watch

The binding constraint is the heavy pair: its propagated tolerance is 0.20%,
the tightest of the five, driven by m_c (0.36%) and m_b (0.17%). Heavy-quark
precision is the near-term lever. m_u at 9.6% is the loosest input and
contributes least, despite being the worst-known mass in the table.
